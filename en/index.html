A charming Provençal residence among the vineyards of Ramatuelle

Tucked away among the vineyards of Ramatuelle, the Auberge de l'Oumède is a peaceful, discreet retreat just minutes from the beaches of Pampelonne and Saint-Tropez. A charming place where comfort, serenity and the gentle Provençal way of life come together, with services designed for an effortless stay.

Before you book

Season

Open from 1 May to 4 October 2026.

Check-in

From 4:00 pm to 8:00 pm. No check-in after 8:00 pm: please let us know your arrival time.

Check-out

From 8:30 am to 11:00 am.

On arrival

Photo ID and a credit card are required.

Children

Welcome. From 2 years old, charged at the adult rate. No extra beds or cots.

Pets

Pets are not allowed.

Quiet hours

Quiet hours from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am. Parties and events are not allowed.

Frequently asked questions

Another question? Call us on 04 94 44 11 11, we answer the phone ourselves.

What are your prices?

From €370 to €1,650 per night depending on the season and the room. The Auberge is open from 1 May to 4 October 2026. Tell us your dates and we will reply with the exact price.

How do I book direct?

Enter your dates in the request form or call us on +33 4 94 44 11 11. We will reply personally with availability and the exact price for your stay.

Is breakfast included?

No, it is available on request (€28 per person). The continental breakfast is served on the terrace, between the pool and the vines.

Is the pool heated?

Yes, the outdoor pool is heated from June. It has a shallow area.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking is available on site. A car is recommended to enjoy the beaches and villages.

Are the beaches easy to reach?

The Auberge is about 1 km from the beaches of Pampelonne, 4 km from the village of Ramatuelle and 6 km from Saint-Tropez. We can help you book your beach.

What is the cancellation policy?

It varies with the room and the period. We will set it out in our reply, before anything is confirmed.

How do I pay on site?

Payment on site is in cash. Other payment methods will be explained when you book.

Are your dates available?

Tell us when you are coming: we will reply personally with availability and the price of each room.
