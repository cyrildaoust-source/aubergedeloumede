Saint-Tropez, Pampelonne and Ramatuelle

Ramatuelle reveals an authentic Provence, minutes from the Auberge. Legendary beaches, a hilltop village, a Provençal market and coastal paths: here are our tips.

Pampelonne beach

We can help you book one of the iconic beaches of the bay of Pampelonne, a true jewel of the Côte d'Azur.

Find out more (external site)

Ramatuelle market

Stroll through the heart of the village and discover the market, its local produce, its colours and its typically Provençal way of life.

Find out more (external site)

Trails & hikes

Explore the paths between the vineyards and the coast, or set off along the coastal trails with unique views over the Mediterranean.

Find out more (external site)

Where to stay for the big events

The Auberge is open from 1 May to 4 October 2026. Book early for these dates.

Ramatuelle Festival

Early August. Open-air theatre, music and comedy in the heart of the village of Ramatuelle, 4 km from the Auberge.

Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez

Late September – early October. The finest classic and modern sailing yachts in the bay. In the evening, back to the calm of the vineyards, far from the crowds at the port.

Provençal markets

All season long. Ramatuelle on Thursdays and Sundays, Saint-Tropez on place des Lices on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Getting here to the Auberge

In a peaceful setting, this typically Provençal residence lies about 1 km from the beaches of Pampelonne, 4 km from the village of Ramatuelle and 6 km from Saint-Tropez. Access via the beach road, chemin de l'Oumède.
