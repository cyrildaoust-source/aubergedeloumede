At the Auberge de l'Oumède, every detail is designed for your comfort: a heated pool, a sunny garden, a terrace overlooking the vineyards, breakfast in the open air and discreet services for a gentle stay.

The gardens

Surrounded by umbrella pines and olive trees, the Auberge opens onto a light-filled Mediterranean garden. Between vineyards as far as the eye can see and shaded corners, every spot invites calm and relaxation, in a gentle, authentic atmosphere where time seems to stand still.
